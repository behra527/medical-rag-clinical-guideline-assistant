How your answer is generated

01

Search and rerankEmbeddings, keyword search, then a second rank that prefers exact terms and numbers. Optional guideline and section filters narrow the search.

02

Drop weak passagesPassages under the similarity threshold are not sent to the model. If none remain, the assistant abstains.

03

Answer with the strict promptThe model may use only the retrieved passages and must cite them as [S1]. Follow-up questions reuse the previous question, not the previous answer, as search context.

04

Check, then label the sourceSentences with unknown citations, numbers, acronyms, or page numbers are removed. The verified PDF page and section are then attached from the index.

Important safety and privacy information

This is an educational and research information tool, not a medical device, diagnostic system, or substitute for a licensed clinician. Do not enter names, birth dates, patient IDs, clinical records, or other personally identifying health information. Queries and document passages are sent to the configured model provider. If you have emergency symptoms, contact local emergency services.

Publishers may impose restrictions on commercial reuse. Verify guideline rights, jurisdiction, publication date, clinical relevance, and version before indexing or deploying content.

Welcome to Veritas Health

Ask a guideline question in English or Urdu. You can limit the search to one indexed guideline or a section such as 1.2, and you can ask a short follow-up. Answers stream in, then each citation is checked.

A citation looks like [S1 p.4 §1.2]. The page and section come from the indexed PDF, not from the model. If no passage is relevant enough, the assistant says so instead of guessing. Saved chats stay in this browser. Evaluation Lab compares top-k, similarity thresholds, and three prompts.
